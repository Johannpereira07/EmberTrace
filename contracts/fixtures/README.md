## Where the numbers come from

They are computed from the **stand-in sample** in `../samples/` (real FIRMS detections, central India, see its README), so `data_kind` is `real`. They are **not product values**: don't present them as results in the demo. They change when Person 2's H2 output replaces the stand-in.

What they cover: all three sources, both handovers, provisional months (2026-08 on), the partial month (2026-11), `zero` months, low counts, and (in `sparse.json`) zero medians giving `not_defined`.

What they **don't** cover, because the stand-in data has none: `unavailable` months (`value: null`), `not_enough_history`, `data_kind: "synthetic"` with the `SYNTHETIC_DATA` warning, and a range starting at 2000-11 (the stand-in starts at 2000-12, and its Terra segment runs to 2002-11). Use the API spec examples (A.1, A.2) for those until H2 or synthetic data lands; the API itself is tested for them in `api/tests/`.
